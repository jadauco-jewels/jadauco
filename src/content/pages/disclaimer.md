We would rather be blunt here than have you disappointed when the parcel opens.

## It is imitation jewellery

Every piece on this site is **brass**, plated in a gold or oxidised silver finish.

- It is **not solid gold**, not gold-filled, and not silver.
- It carries **no hallmark**, because there is nothing to hallmark.
- It has **no resale or investment value**. Buy it to wear, not to keep.

## The stones are not precious

Where a piece is described as having stones, those are **glass, resin, cubic zirconia or
imitation kundan**. Nothing here contains a diamond, a ruby, an emerald, a sapphire or a real
pearl, whatever the design is imitating.

We use words like *kundan*, *temple*, *polki*, *American diamond* and *pearl* because those are
the names of the **styles**. They describe what a piece looks like, not what it is made of.

## The finish wears

Plating is a coating, and coatings come off. Perfume, sweat, water, hairspray and friction all
take it off faster.

**Regular wear will show wear and tear**, starting at the contact points. How soon depends
entirely on how a piece is worn and stored, and it varies too much between people for us to put
a figure on it — so we do not, and we give **no warranty on finish**. Our
[Care guide](/care-guide/) is the best way to make it last.

## Photographs and descriptions

Photographs are taken in daylight against a plain ground. Jewellery photographs brighter than it
wears, and colours vary between screens.

Measurements and weights are accurate to within normal manufacturing tolerance and may vary
slightly between batches. Stone placement varies a little on handmade pieces. If exact dimensions
matter to you, ask us before ordering and we will measure the actual piece.

## Product names

Names describe a style, not an origin, a maker or a brand. Nothing here is affiliated with, or
endorsed by, any jeweller, designer or house whose style a piece may resemble.

## Skin and allergies

Every piece is plated brass, and some people react to plated metals. We do not test our pieces
for nickel content and we make no claim about it either way, so if you have sensitive skin or a
known metal allergy, please patch-test before wearing anything for a full day. We cannot accept
responsibility for a reaction.

## Shipping

We dispatch within two working days of payment confirmation. Delivery is typically three to six
working days across India, longer for the North East and remote pin codes.

Every order ships with a tracking number, sent over the same WhatsApp thread you ordered on.

Pieces are wrapped individually in bubble film and pouches, then boxed — bangles and stone-set
necklaces travel well; what does not travel well is a loose piece in an envelope, which is why
we do not ship that way even when it would be cheaper.

## If something arrives damaged

Send us photographs on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery, along with a photograph of the
outer packaging. We will replace the piece or refund it in full, our choice of courier, and you
will not pay return shipping.

**Please film or photograph the parcel as you open it.** For a damaged-in-transit claim it is
the difference between a same-day replacement and a long conversation.

## If it is not what you expected

Tell us within 48 hours and return it unworn, in its original packaging, and we will refund the
piece price. Return shipping is yours in this case. Refunds are processed within five working
days of the parcel reaching us.

We would much rather you ask first. Send a message before ordering and we will send more
photographs, the exact weight, or a video of the piece in daylight — jewellery photographs
brighter than it wears, and that gap is the most common reason for a return.

## What cannot be returned

Earrings, for hygiene reasons, unless they arrive damaged or are the wrong item. This is
standard across the industry and we are afraid we cannot make an exception.

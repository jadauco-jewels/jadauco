A bracelet is the easiest piece to get right, because almost everything here closes on a lobster
clasp with two or three inches of extender chain. If a bangle in 2.4 is tight and 2.6 slides off,
this is the shelf to look at instead.

The collection runs from fine CZ tennis chains that sit flat under a shirt cuff, through kundan
and meenakari straps meant to be seen alongside a lehenga, to solid oxidised cuffs that open at
the side and need no clasp at all. Cuffs are the one exception to the sizing above — they are a
free size with a little give, and a slim wrist is better served by a chain.

Every piece is brass under the plating, finished in gold or antique polish, so the
[care guide](/care-guide/) applies here as much as anywhere: off before a bath, dry before the
box. Send us the code on WhatsApp and we will tell you the exact length of the chain and what
the extender adds before you order.

A pendant is the piece to buy when a full necklace is more than the occasion asks for. Ours are
sold with a chain, and the chain length is on every product page — 16 inches sits at the collar
bone, 18 inches sits just below it, and both are adjustable by an inch at the clasp.

The range covers small kundan and CZ solitaires light enough to leave on all day, temple motifs
in antique gold for festive wear, and a few larger statement drops meant to be seen against a
plain kurta or a solid saree. Weight is the thing to watch: anything under 15 grams with its
chain will not pull at the neck by evening, and the specification table lists it for every
piece.

Where a pendant is available without its chain — because you already have one you like — the
page says so. Ask over WhatsApp and we will price it either way.

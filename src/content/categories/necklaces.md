Our necklace collection runs from kundan bridal chokers heavy enough to carry a wedding
lehenga, through temple-work haars in antique gold, to light everyday chains you can wear to
the office and forget you have on.

Every piece is built on a base metal — usually brass — and finished with a gold or antique
polish. Like any plating it wears with use; the [care guide](/care-guide/) is what makes the
difference. Chokers come with an adjustable dori at the back, so sizing is rarely a problem —
and where a set includes matching earrings or a maang tikka, the specification table on the
product page says exactly what arrives in the box.

If you are shopping for a wedding, start with the bridal sets and give yourself time: the
photographs matter more than the description, and we are happy to send more of them over
WhatsApp before you decide.

Bangles are sold by size, and the size is the one thing worth getting right before you order.
Ours run from 2.4 through 2.10, and every listing states its size in the specification table.
If you are unsure, measure a bangle you already wear across its inner diameter and tell us the
number over WhatsApp — it takes a minute and saves an exchange.

The collection covers cubic zirconia stone sets that catch light under photography, meenakari
work in traditional enamel colours, and plain polished kadas that stack with anything. Sets of
four and six are common for festive wear; singles work better as an everyday piece alongside a
watch.

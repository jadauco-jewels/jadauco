Most of our rings are adjustable, which removes the single biggest reason jewellery bought
online goes back unworn. An adjustable shank opens and closes to fit somewhere between a size 12
and a size 18, so a ring bought as a gift will almost always fit.

The range covers cocktail rings large enough to stand alone against a plain saree, kundan
statement pieces that match the bridal sets in the necklace collection, and slim cubic zirconia
bands that sit comfortably under everyday wear.

Where a ring is fixed-size rather than adjustable, its page says so plainly in the
specification table.

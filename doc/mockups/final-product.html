About this set

A five-line kundan choker with a matching pair of jhumkas, made on a brass base and finished in gold. The stones are imitation kundan set in a raised bezel, with glass pearls hanging along the lower edge — the shape sits high on the collarbone, which is why it works under a heavy dupatta without disappearing.

It closes on an adjustable dori rather than a hook, so it fits most necks and does not need resizing. The earrings have nickel-free posts and are light enough to wear through a full evening. Total weight for the set is 120 grams.

Kept dry and stored in its pouch, the gold finish holds for around eighteen months of regular wear. Put it on after perfume and hairspray, not before. Full care guide →

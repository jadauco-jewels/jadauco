About this set

A pendant and matching earrings on a brass base, finished in gold. Four arcs of American diamond — round stones on two rows, rectangular baguettes on the others — loop over and cross at the waist, then fan out into six cream glass pearls with a larger pearl hanging at the centre. The crossing is what gives the piece its depth: the stones catch light at different angles rather than all at once.

The pendant has a 4 mm bail and takes any standard chain; a chain is not included, so it works with one you already own. The earrings are the same design at roughly half scale, on nickel-free posts. Together the set weighs 42 grams, which is light enough for a full evening.

Kept dry and stored in its pouch, the gold finish holds for around eighteen months of regular wear. Put it on after perfume and hairspray, not before. Full care guide →

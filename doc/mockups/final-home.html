What you're actually buying

The metal

A brass base, plated in a gold or oxidised silver finish. No solid gold, no precious stones — that is the point. It looks like the set you'd be nervous to travel with, and costs what it costs.

The fit

Nickel-free posts and hooks throughout. Chokers on adjustable dori, kadas in 2.4 and 2.6 inch, haars with a three-inch extender. Every measurement is on the product page.

The upkeep

Keep it dry, keep it in its pouch, put it on after the perfume. Treated that way the finish holds for about eighteen months of regular wear.

Four directions for the jewellery catalogue

Same content model, same WhatsApp-enquiry mechanic, four different personalities. Each one is a working homepage — open it, resize it, hover things. Colour and type in every file sit in a single token block at the top, which maps straight onto src/styles/tokens.css from the plan.

The directions

All four now carry the chosen name, so only the design differs

V1Vitrine

Shown as “Jadauco”

A dark jeweller's showcase. Pieces lit out of near-black with a gold spotlight and a reflection on the pedestal. Quiet, expensive-feeling, aimed at bridal buyers.

Signature
A brass SKU tag clipped to every piece — the code is the product
Type
Rozha One (Devanagari-derived display) + Mukta + Khand
Risk
Dark background; needs good photography to carry it

Swarnayaswar-NAH-ya From swarna, Sanskrit for gold. Reads premium, works in every Indian language. swarnaya.com free Nothing found. “Swarna” alone is used widely, so keep the full form.
Meenakiyamee-na-KEE-ya From meenakari, the enamel craft. Says exactly what the jewellery looks like. meenakiya.com free Nothing found. “Meena” is common in the trade; the coined ending protects it.
RupmatiROOP-ma-tee Rani Rupmati of Mandu; roop = beauty, form. Historic, feminine, easy to spell. rupmati.com free No jewellery brand found. Used as a personal name and a place name.
Niraliyani-RAA-li-ya From nirali — one of a kind, unlike the rest. Says the positioning out loud. niraliya.com free Nothing found.
Meenayamee-NAH-ya Softer, shorter cousin of Meenakiya. Easier to say on a phone call. meenaya.com free Meena Jewellers exists (Dubai/US, fine jewellery). Different market, but check IP India.
PolkiyaPOL-ki-ya From polki, uncut-stone work. Strongest SEO hook of the set. polkiya.com free Free, but ties the brand to one style — awkward if the range broadens.
JhilmiliJHIL-mi-lee Jhilmil = shimmer, twinkle. The warmest, most memorable of the lot. jhilmili.com free Jhilmil Jewels (Chennai, wholesale) and a Jhilmil Jewellery page exist. Usable, but do a TM search first.
Suchi Mukhiformer working name Reads as shuchi = pure + mukhi = faced. But suchi-mukhi also parses as “needle-faced”, and Sucimukhi is a demoness in the epics. suchimukhi.com taken Dropped. The second reading was the deciding factor.
Jadaucoja-DAO-co From jadau — the Mughal technique of embedding uncut stones in molten gold — plus the trade-house co. Names the craft, not the category. jadauco.com yours Chosen. Still to do: IP India Class 14 search and the Instagram handle.

Decided: Jadauco

The shortlist above is kept as a record of how we got here. Jadauco was chosen and jadauco.com registered; the four directions below are now all shown under that name.

Two things a registered domain does not cover, both still open: run the name through IP India's public trademark search under Class 14 (jewellery), and claim the Instagram handle — for this business the handle matters more than the domain.

Mocks are static HTML with no build step. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so open them online. Jewellery images are drawn as SVG placeholders — they get replaced by real photography.

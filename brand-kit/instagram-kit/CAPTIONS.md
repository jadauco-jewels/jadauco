# Captions

## Pattern

    Name.
    Material and finish.
    Price.
    One line on care or styling.
    Call to action.

## Example

    Lakshmi temple set.
    Brass with a gold finish, ruby-red stone at the centre. Nickel-free posts.
    ₹1,299 for the necklace and studs.
    Wipe it dry and keep it in the pouch — the finish lasts much longer that way.
    Link in bio.

## Rules

Say brass. Say cubic zirconia. Say gold finish, never gold.
Never use the word hallmark. Never imply a piece is solid gold or stone-set.

The brand's honesty about being imitation is what makes the tagline land:
"Real enough to wear every day."

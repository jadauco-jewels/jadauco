# Templates

01  PRODUCT           Full-bleed photo, gradient at the foot, name + price.
                      Your default post. Works with any shot.

02  NEW ARRIVAL       Label band above, photo centre, name and price below.
                      For a piece that needs breathing room.

03  EDIT / COLLECTION Three photos plus one gold title block.
                      Drops and festive collections.

04  SPEC              Photo above, three spec columns below (metal, stones,
                      posts) plus name and price. Answers DM questions.

05  TAGLINE           Dimmed product shot behind the line. One post in eight.

06  OFFER             Gold border, big percentage, code. The only bordered
                      template, so discounts never look like product posts.

07  CARE / TIPS       Three numbered lines on black. Saves well.
                      Reuse the structure for styling tips.

08  REVIEW            Italic quote, first name and city, dimmed shot behind.
                      Real reviews only.

09  STORY             1080x1920. Photo full-bleed, type in the middle band,
                      mark at the foot.

10  REEL COVER        1080x1920. Mark, title, duration, centred so the crop
                      still works as a grid thumbnail.

# Jadauco — guidelines

## The mark

A ring seen face-on with the stone set at twelve o'clock. It is geometry, not an
illustration: a circle, a stroke, and a rotated square. That is why it survives
at favicon size and on a printed pouch.

The notch behind the stone is filled with the background colour, not white. If
you place the mark on sand, the notch is sand. Getting this wrong leaves a white
halo.

## Lockups

Primary (stacked) — mark over wordmark over descriptor. Use on the box lid,
the loading screen, and anywhere the logo is the only thing on the surface.

Horizontal — mark left, wordmark right. Use in the site header, invoices, and
email signatures. Drop the descriptor below 26px wordmark size.

Mark alone — favicon, app icon, social avatar, packaging pattern. Never pair it
with a retyped "Jadauco" set in some other font.

## Voice

Plain and specific. Name the material, the finish and the price. Say what a
piece is made of and how to look after it.

Write:      Brass base with a gold finish. Nickel-free posts.
Don't:      Timeless luxury, crafted to perfection.

Never imply the pieces are solid gold or stone-set, and never use the word
hallmark. The brand's honesty about being imitation is the reason the tagline
works.

## Photography

Cream or sand backgrounds. Soft daylight, no hard specular hits. One piece per
frame for the grid, plus one on-body shot per product so scale is obvious.
Avoid black velvet and jewel-toned silk: they signal fine jewellery and set the
wrong price expectation.

## Layout

Generous whitespace. Sand blocks separate sections; gold appears once per
screen at most. Product grids are three across on desktop, two on mobile, with
the price in mono directly under the name.
